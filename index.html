Privacy Policy

Weld Shop Tracker is a locally operated production-scheduling application for Windows.

Information you enter

You may enter job or customer names, production dates, weld counts, production steps, allotted and actual labor hours, completed quantities, and notes. This information is stored on the device in the app's local WebView2 browser storage, associated with the current Windows user profile.

How information is used and shared

The app uses the information you enter only to display and manage your production schedules and progress. The app does not send those records to the developer, operate an account or cloud service, or provide automatic synchronization or sharing between devices or Windows user accounts. The developer does not sell or share your entered production information.

Anyone with access to your Windows account or device may be able to access locally stored app data. Windows or device backup tools that you enable may also include app data; those tools are governed by their providers' terms and privacy practices.

Storage and deletion

Your records remain in the app's local storage until they are cleared or removed through device storage management. Uninstalling the app may not automatically remove this data. The app does not provide an automatic cloud backup.

Third-party software

The Windows app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2 to display its interface. Microsoft and Windows may process technical or diagnostic information under their own privacy terms and settings. The developer does not receive your production records through WebView2.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The latest version will be published at this address.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support or contact information on the Weld Shop Tracker Microsoft Store listing.
